Vascular atlas

Three-dimensional visualizations of the heart, neck and brain vasculature. Every scene draws on the SPL Head & Neck Atlas, the JHU parcellation and BrainCharter data; the space is RAS, in mm. The info boxes note that the scan is cut at z ≈ −160 mm and that the JHU alignment is approximate (scale 0.865, rotation 3.6°, median residual 1.2 mm).

Scenes that traverse the whole pathway or show several systems together.

The full tour of the vessels running from the heart up the neck to the skull base: starting at the aortic arch, labeled with JHU regions. You can adjust the opacity of the bone, vessel and soft-tissue layers with sliders.
