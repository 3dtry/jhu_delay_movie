3D rendering of the JHU arterial territory delay map. Regions are colored by their group-average bolus-arrival delay, from the earliest (MCA territories, purple, around −0.5 s) to the latest (deep internal capsule regions, yellow, up to +0.92 s). The panel on the right lists the 30 regions in delay order; the bar at the bottom shows the current time marker sliding through the delay range.
